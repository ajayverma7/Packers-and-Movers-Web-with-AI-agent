THE MOVEMATE GUIDE

Small details make moving day lighter.

Keep these practical notes close while you plan, pack and settle into your next address.

Your moving-week plan

Confirm access times, reserve lift space and finish non-essential packing first.

  • 7 days out: confirm date, address and building rules
  • 3 days out: pack an essentials bag and label rooms
  • 1 day out: photograph connections and meter readings

Pack room by room

Give each carton one destination and one clear label.

  • Use small boxes for books and dense items
  • Mark fragile boxes on two sides
  • Keep medicines, chargers and documents with you

Plan the long route

Long-distance moves feel easier when the first 24 hours are planned separately.

  • Carry valuables and important documents personally
  • Share a reachable phone number with the move team
  • Keep food, water and a change of clothes ready

Ask about protection

Ask what is covered and how inventory is recorded before booking.

  • Request the item inventory in writing
  • Photograph valuable or delicate items
  • Keep the delivery confirmation safely

Make the first night easy

Pack one clearly marked “open first” carton with the basics.

  • Toiletries, towels and a change of clothes
  • Tea, snacks, chargers and basic tools
  • Sheets, cleaning supplies and pet essentials

Moving across Hyderabad

A confirmed parking and lift slot can save time on moving day.

  • Check society loading hours in advance
  • Measure narrow gates and elevator doors
  • Plan a covered loading point during monsoon
